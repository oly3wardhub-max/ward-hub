Set up Ward Hub

For a ward that wants its own copy. About 20 minutes. Each ward's copy is completely separate: no data is shared, and none is stored in this app's code.

1 · The spreadsheet (your ward's data)
  1. Sign in to Google with a ward account (for example, a Gmail made for the executive secretary).
  2. Make a new, empty Google Sheet. Name it Ward Hub.
  3. Choose Extensions › Apps Script. Delete what's there and paste everything from Engine.gs. Click Save.
  4. Pick setUp in the function list and click Run. Allow access when Google asks. Every tab is created for you.
  5. On the Settings tab, fill in the Value column (ward name, bishop, executive secretary…).
2 · Turn on the engine
  1. In Apps Script: Deploy › New deployment › Web app. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy and copy the Web app URL.
  2. Paste that URL into Settings › Engine address.
3 · The app
  1. Make a free GitHub account, then fork (copy) this app's repository to it.
  2. In your copy: Settings › Pages › Deploy from a branch › main › Save. Your app's address appears there.
  3. Put your app address in Settings › App address, and (optional) your engine URL in config.js.
  4. Back in the spreadsheet, choose Ward Hub › Show my admin link and open it on your phone.
  5. Add people on the Access tab (name and email). From the app, send each person their personal link.

Paste LCR lists (members, callings) from the app's Paste screen to fill People and Callings.
